# XONEXI

A third-person hack-and-slash in the style of Dynasty Warriors, set in the Mirror Universe in the year 22789 HR. It runs in the browser.

Emperor Aedanius I Cretacea has proclaimed himself a god. From his throne on the space station Archiostal, near Malana in the Plazith Rim, Archamus the Prime Evil declares war on the Gigaquadrantic Hegemony. He strikes first at the capital, Xonexi. There he breaks the Bunsen terrorists, then Tirochonadral, the Emperor's jailer and protector, together with Patriarch Uriel Ultanos of Dranvamus, and finally the false god himself.

## Play

Double-click `index.html` to play, voices included (they are embedded in `voices.js`).

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left stick |
| Attack (4-hit chain) | Left click / J | Attack |
| Charge attack | Right click / K | Charge |
| Dodge | Space / Shift | Dodge |
| Eclipse of Archiostal (Musou) | L / F | Eclipse |
| Camera | Mouse (click to lock), Q/E, wheel | Drag right side |
| Pause | Esc / P | II |

## Contents

- `index.html`: the game, with its own small WebGL renderer
- `archamus.js`, `aedanius.js`, `tirochonadral.js`, `uriel.js`: the character models, embedded for the game
- `models/`: the original `.glb` models
- `audio/`: voice lines (`INTRO1–3`, `FIGHT1–13`, `URIEL1–4`); `voices.js` embeds them so the game works offline
